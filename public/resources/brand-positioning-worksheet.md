# Brand positioning worksheet

1. Audience: Who needs your offer most? What do they want to change?
2. Category: Which alternatives do they compare you with?
3. Promise: What specific outcome can you credibly deliver?
4. Evidence: List three concrete reasons to believe your promise.
5. Difference: What makes your approach distinctive and useful?
6. Message: Complete: For [audience], [brand] delivers [outcome] through [difference], backed by [evidence].
7. Identity: Which words, images, and design choices support that message?
8. Action: Select one touchpoint to improve this week. Record the owner, deadline, and success measure.
